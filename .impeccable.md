## Design Context

### Users
People who want to open a chess site, press Start, and play a complete game against Stockfish. The requested stack is Next.js and Tailwind CSS, contained in `code/`.

### Brand Personality
Calm, thoughtful, welcoming. The visual direction below is an implementation assumption, offered to the user while setup continued.

### Aesthetic Direction
A classic chess club: warm cream, forest green, soft sage board squares, editorial serif headings, and a precise sans-serif interface. The board is the focal point, with a restrained setup panel alongside it.

### Design Principles
- Make starting a game obvious and keep the board usable on small screens.
- Explain turns, legal moves, and engine states clearly.
- Use real interactions and honest game information.
- Support keyboard play, touch, and reduced motion.
- Keep visual ornament quiet and purposeful.
